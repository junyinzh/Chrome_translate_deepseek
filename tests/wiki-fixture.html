Riverton[a] is a small town near the Alpine foothills, where the River Alder meets a mountain lake. Its historic center contains a railway station, a public library and a market square at 1,800 metres (5,910 ft) above sea level.

The southern slopes overlook a broad valley. Visitors arrive by train during the winter season.
